Region War - Free Multiplayer Browser Strategy Game

Region War is a free multiplayer strategy game that runs in your browser. Pick a country, fight wars to conquer regions on a world map, and build a national economy together with other players.

Game features

  • War: attack or defend regions, and join wars on either side with other players.
  • World map: every region has its own name and resources, and belongs to a country.
  • National economy: PvE income and territory production feed each country's national treasury.
  • PvP and PvE: fight other players or the game, level up, and improve your attack, defense, speed and critical chance.
  • Market, crafting and inventory: trade resources, craft items and manage your equipment.
  • Kingdom and rankings: work with your country and climb the leaderboards.
  • Shop: customize your profile with cosmetics.

How to play

Create a free account, choose your country, then play from the map and war screens. No download is needed: Region War works on desktop and mobile browsers. Please enable JavaScript to play.
